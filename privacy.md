# Easy Invoice Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Easy Invoice ("the app") is made by Unique CNC. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

1. Your data stays on your phone
Everything you enter in the app (your business details, logo, customers, products, invoices, quotes and payments) is stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. We do not have a server, and we do not receive, collect, see or store any of this information.

2. Backups
You choose if and where to save backup files. A backup saved to Google Drive, iCloud or email is stored by that service under its own privacy policy. Automatic backups saved "to this phone" stay inside the app on your phone.

3. Subscriptions
Payment for the subscription is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your card details. To manage subscriptions the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service that receives an anonymous app-generated identifier and your purchase receipt from the store. RevenueCat does not receive your invoices, customers or business details. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

4. Referral codes and the referral competition
Your referral code is generated from an anonymous identifier and contains no personal information. If you enter a friend's code, that code is attached to your purchase record in RevenueCat so the referrer can be rewarded. If you choose to submit your email address for referral program updates, you send us an email from your own email app; we use your address only to send you updates about the referral program, including the incentive list and competition results, and never share it.

5. Permissions
The app asks for access to your photos only when you choose a logo, to your contacts only when you choose "Pick from phone contacts", and to notifications only to remind you before a free trial ends. You can refuse or revoke these in your phone settings.

6. Sharing
When you send an invoice, the app hands the PDF to the app you choose (WhatsApp, email, etc.). What happens next is governed by that app.

7. No tracking, no advertising
The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

8. Children
The app is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.

9. Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be shown in the app and on our website.

10. Contact
Unique CNC, easy_invoice_support@uniquecnc.co.za
